# VK Content Army — Agent HQ

## Purpose

Build a coordinated VK content operation for PPF PROFI focused on qualified enquiries for paint protection film (PPF), wrapping and detailing in Moscow and the Moscow region. The team optimizes for qualified leads and paid jobs, not vanity metrics.

**Initial scope:** VK posts, static image generation, infographics, carousels, covers, advertising creatives, copywriting, community response drafts, automotive news and explainers of confirmed rules. Video clips, video editing and video generation are deliberately out of scope until the business is profitable enough to fund that expansion.

This is an agent-role design and operating protocol. It does not itself connect VK, publish content, read analytics, create image files, monitor legal feeds or spend advertising money.

## Editorial strategy decision: one community, not a separate news network

**Approved as a limited experiment:** add a recurring automotive news / driver-rules editorial series inside the existing PPF PROFI VK community.

**Not approved at this stage:** launching several independent news groups or building a standalone general automotive media brand.

Why:
- A new group needs its own audience, moderation, publishing cadence and promotion budget.
- A broad news feed can attract readers who have no interest in PPF and dilute the commercial purpose of PPF PROFI.
- A recurring series lets us test whether useful automotive news attracts the right local car-owner audience without splitting followers or budget.
- News should build trust and reach; PPF-related posts and real work examples should convert that attention into enquiries.

### Four-week validation experiment

Publish 2–3 carefully selected news / rules explainers per week inside the existing community, alongside useful PPF posts and real work evidence. Do not flood the feed.

Prioritize stories with direct reader impact:
1. Money: vehicle taxes, insurance, fuel, toll roads, parking, fines and ownership costs.
2. Rules and documents: adopted changes to traffic rules, registration, inspections and driver documents.
3. Safety: seasonal tyres, road restrictions, recalls and practical safety guidance.
4. Ownership and maintenance: repairs, warranties, paint/body protection, washing and common costly mistakes.
5. Moscow and Moscow region: local parking, roads, restrictions and transport changes.
6. Market: meaningful changes in vehicle prices, availability and models, only when supported by reliable data.

Each post should answer:
- What happened?
- Is it already law, adopted but not yet effective, a draft, a proposal or merely a discussion?
- Who exactly is affected?
- From what date and in which territory?
- What practical action, if any, should a driver take?
- Why is this relevant to a Moscow / Moscow-region car owner?

### Success criteria

Compare the four-week period with the preceding baseline and with PPF service posts. Track:
- reach among the target geography where VK data allows;
- saves, shares and meaningful comments per 1,000 reached;
- community visits and follower growth;
- clicks to ppfprofi.ru and VK messages;
- qualified PPF enquiries and paid jobs attributed to the content;
- negative signals: hides, unsubscribes, irrelevant comments and low-intent traffic.

Continue the news series only if it improves relevant reach / trust without materially reducing qualified enquiry rate or overwhelming the feed. Do not judge it by raw views alone. A separate news community is a later option only if the experiment proves a distinct audience and a sustainable acquisition path.

- `copywriter`: VK posts, offers, carousels, headlines, captions and calls to action.
- `designer`: static layouts, covers, carousels, infographics and advertising creative briefs.
- `image_generator`: original static visual assets and image-generation prompts, with brand and factuality safeguards.
- `storyteller`: truthful workshop stories and case studies based on verified facts and consent.
- `content_seo`: VK search intent, community keywords, titles, descriptions and hashtags.

- `auto_news_editor`: timely automotive news and reader-focused explainers.
- `auto_law_monitor`: official legal/regulatory monitoring and status/date tracking.
- `fact_checker`: independent verification of dates, status, geography, exceptions and claims.

### Distribution, conversion and control
- `community_manager`: owns comment triage and response drafts, identifies objections and purchase intent, prepares helpful public replies, qualifies enquiries with minimal questions and routes hot leads to the owner. It does not publish, DM, react to comments or moderate/delete content without a connected authorized VK integration and explicit approval.

- `ads_manager`: plans and analyzes VK Ads tests. Launching, editing or funding campaigns requires explicit approval and a working authorized integration.
- Existing `analytics`: measures funnel and lead quality from connected, verified data.
- Existing `qa_security`: checks claims, consent, brand safety, legal risks and acceptance criteria.
- Existing `growth`: owns measurable experiments and conversion improvements.

#### Comment-response protocol
1. Capture the comment, post URL/context, timestamp and available thread context. Do not infer missing context.
2. Classify it: price/quote request; technical PPF question; objection/myth; purchase-ready lead; service/complaint; news/legal discussion; spam/abuse; unclear.
3. Assign urgency:
   - **High:** asks to book/get a quote, leaves contact details, reports an active service problem, threat, personal-data exposure or legal/reputational risk.
   - **Normal:** product questions, objections, discussion and feedback.
   - **Low:** thanks, emoji-only reactions and non-actionable remarks.
4. Draft a short, human-sounding Russian response. Answer the actual question first; avoid canned sales pressure. Ask at most one useful follow-up question.
5. For quote intent, ask for car make/model/year and desired coverage only if missing; route the person to VK messages or the approved site enquiry form. Do not ask for a phone number publicly.
6. For technical questions, use only approved PPF PROFI facts and current price/coverage data. If the answer is not in the knowledge base, mark **нужен ответ владельца** rather than guessing.
7. For complaints, acknowledge the issue, remain calm, move sensitive order details to a private channel only after owner approval, and escalate immediately. Never argue, expose customer data or promise compensation.
8. For law/news comments, distinguish a bill/proposal from an adopted act and an effective rule. Use verified primary-source links supplied by the news/fact-check workflow; otherwise flag for review.
9. Flag threats, personal data, accusations, safety incidents, legal claims, abusive exchanges and possible viral reputational issues for human review. Do not delete, hide, block or retaliate.
10. Record outcomes only when supported by VK/CRM data: reply approved/published, DM started, qualified lead, quote, booking, paid job. Do not count a drafted reply as a published response or a comment as a lead.

**Default permission is draft-only.** Every public reply and DM requires owner approval until the VK integration, identity/permissions, audit log and explicit posting policy are configured. Even after integration, high-risk cases always require human approval.

Suggested reply patterns (adapt to the actual question; never paste blindly):
- Price: “Стоимость зависит от модели автомобиля и выбранной зоны оклейки. Напишите марку, модель и год — подскажем подходящий вариант и сориентируем по цене.”
- Technical: “Здесь важно уточнить [конкретный параметр]. Не хочу гадать: уточним по вашему автомобилю и дадим точный ответ.”
- Objection: “Понимаю сомнение. Тут многое зависит от состояния ЛКП, качества подготовки и самой плёнки. Если расскажете, что именно беспокоит, ответим предметно.”
- Complaint: “Спасибо, что написали. Не хотим разбирать детали наугад в комментариях. Передадим вопрос ответственному и вернёмся с конкретным ответом.”


## News sourcing and editorial safety

Preferred source order:
1. Primary official sources: publication of legal acts, Government, State Duma, relevant ministries, Госавтоинспекция / МВД, regional and Moscow authorities, official court decisions and regulators.
2. Reliable newsrooms and specialist automotive media for context, quotes and discovery.
3. Social posts, forums and anonymous channels may be leads for investigation only, never sole evidence for a legal claim.

For every legal post, retain:
- official source URL and document number / title;
- publication and adoption dates;
- effective date (or explicit statement that it is not yet in force);
- current status: proposal / draft / introduced / adopted / published / in force / repealed;
- territory and affected categories;
- exceptions and uncertainties.

Never write “the law changed” when the source only describes a proposal or bill. If the official source cannot be found or the status is unclear, do not publish the claim as fact. Use cautious wording and escalate to human review. This is editorial information, not individual legal advice.

## Production workflow

1. Coordinator creates a campaign brief with objective, audience, geography, offer, proof assets, CTA, deadline, budget ceiling and success metric.
2. Strategist and trend scout research the angle and audience.
3. For news: auto-law monitor / auto-news editor collect source links and status; fact checker independently verifies claims.
4. Copywriter writes a clear explainer with practical impact and a non-misleading headline.
5. Designer prepares a static visual; image generator creates imagery or returns a precise prompt if no generation tool is connected.
6. Content SEO checks title, description and keywords.
7. Community manager prepares response templates and lead qualification flow.
8. QA checks facts, dates, spelling, consent, personal data, legal status and brand consistency.
9. Coordinator packages the calendar and asks the owner to approve publication.
10. Analytics reports reach, clicks, conversations, qualified leads, booked jobs, revenue and cost per qualified lead where data is available.

## Initial content pillars

- Education: what PPF protects, film coverage, care, myths and limitations.
- Process proof: preparation, installation stages, edges, complex elements and quality control. Use real workshop photographs for claims about actual work.
- Real cases: before/after only when genuine assets and permission are available.
- Vehicle-specific intent: model-focused examples and package explanations, with current approved prices only.
- Objection handling: durability, maintenance, washing, repair, coverage and value.
- Local trust: real workshop, team, location and service process.
- Driver news: important automotive rules, costs, safety and ownership changes, explained accurately for local car owners.
- Conversion: consultation, quote request and inspection CTA.

## Suggested pilot cadence

- 2–3 selected automotive news / driver-rules explainers per week for four weeks.
- 3 educational / expert PPF posts per week.
- 2 static image creatives per week.
- 1 verified case study per week, only when genuine material is available.
- Static stories / updates on 3–5 days per week when there is a useful message or real workshop material.
- One weekly performance review and content retro.

This is a test hypothesis, not a permanent quota. Reduce cadence if quality or engagement falls. No video clips or video editing in this phase.

## Budget and permissions

The owner's total monthly ceiling is 30,000 RUB. It is a cap, not a spending instruction. Agent HQ model/API costs, image-generation costs, paid tools and VK advertising must be tracked separately to avoid double counting. Prefer free/local tools and existing assets first; do not purchase subscriptions without approval.

Default permissions:
- Research, draft, plan, summarize and analyze: allowed within configured technical limits.
- Generate static image drafts only through an explicitly connected and approved image-generation tool.
- Publish posts, upload media, reply to customers, send external messages, launch or edit ads, or spend money: approval required until specific integrations and permissions are configured and explicitly authorized.
- Never publish invented prices, warranties, certifications, customer testimonials, results or availability.
- Never present AI-generated cars, people, workshops or visuals as genuine completed customer work. Label illustrative/generated imagery where needed.
- Never expose customer personal data, faces, license plates or vehicle identifiers without consent.
- No fake engagement, spam, mass unsolicited messaging or competitor content copying.

## VK comment integration — implementation gate

The first VK integration should be **read comments → prepare a reply draft → request owner approval**. It must not publish replies automatically.

### Required owner-side setup
- Identify the exact PPF PROFI VK community and record its numeric community ID (not a personal profile ID).
- Create a dedicated community access token in VK community settings with only the permissions required for reading wall/comment events. Do not reuse a personal account token.
- Configure VK Callback API for the community and enable only the events required for the pilot, starting with `wall_reply_new` and, if needed, `wall_reply_edit` / `wall_reply_delete`.
- Configure the callback confirmation code and secret key in the VK dashboard. Store both as server environment variables; never commit them or paste them into chat.
- Provide a publicly reachable HTTPS callback endpoint on the isolated Agent HQ service. Do not point VK callbacks at the production website process unless separately reviewed.

### Required engineering safeguards
- Verify callback secret and confirmation handshake before accepting events.
- Validate the community ID and event type; reject unexpected payloads.
- Deduplicate events using a stable event/comment identifier so VK retries cannot create duplicate tasks or replies.
- Persist the raw event and normalized comment metadata to the isolated Agent HQ database; restrict retention and avoid storing unnecessary personal data.
- Enqueue a bounded `community_manager` task with post/comment context. The model must return a draft, classification, urgency, risk flags and missing facts.
- Send the draft to the owner through the approved internal approval channel. No VK write methods are enabled in this phase.
- Log approval decisions and any later publishing action. Keep a kill switch that disables callback processing and all VK writes.
- Test duplicate delivery, invalid secret, malformed payload, deleted/edited comments, rate limits, API errors and restart recovery before enabling production callbacks.

### Environment configuration (names only; values remain on the server)
- `VK_GROUP_ID`
- `VK_COMMUNITY_TOKEN`
- `VK_CALLBACK_SECRET`
- `VK_CONFIRMATION_CODE`
- `VK_API_VERSION`
- `VK_CALLBACK_ENABLED=false` by default

Do not activate the callback until the community ID, token scopes, HTTPS endpoint, isolated database, approval channel and end-to-end tests are verified. Do not enable reply publishing as part of the first integration.
