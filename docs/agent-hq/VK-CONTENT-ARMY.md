# VK Content Army — Agent HQ

## Purpose

Build a coordinated content operation for PPF PROFI focused on qualified enquiries for paint protection film (PPF), wrapping and detailing in Moscow and the Moscow region. The team optimizes for qualified leads and paid jobs, not vanity metrics.

This is an agent-role design and operating protocol. It does not itself connect VK, publish content, read analytics, create media files or spend advertising money.

## Roles

The coordinator owns strategy, decomposes campaigns into tasks, assigns specialists, reconciles outputs and requests approvals for restricted actions.

### Editorial and creative team
- `vk_strategist`: audience segments, positioning, content pillars, weekly calendar and campaign briefs.
- `copywriter`: VK posts, offers, carousels, headlines and CTAs.
- `reels_creator`: short vertical video concepts, hooks, scripts, shot lists and captions.
- `video_producer`: editing briefs based on supplied footage and approved scripts.
- `designer`: visual concepts and production briefs for covers, carousels and infographics.
- `storyteller`: truthful workshop stories and case studies based on verified facts and consent.
- `content_seo`: VK search intent, community keywords, titles, descriptions and hashtags.
- `trend_scout`: relevant automotive / PPF / detailing trends and competitor observations.

### Distribution, conversion and control
- `community_manager`: drafts comment and DM replies, qualifies enquiries and routes high-intent leads. Sending messages requires an authorized integration and approval.
- `ads_manager`: plans and analyzes VK Ads tests. Launching, editing or funding campaigns requires explicit approval and a working authorized integration.
- Existing `analytics`: measures funnel and lead quality from connected, verified data.
- Existing `qa_security`: checks claims, consent, brand safety, legal risks and acceptance criteria.
- Existing `growth`: owns measurable experiments and conversion improvements.

## Production workflow

1. Coordinator creates a campaign brief with objective, audience, geography, offer, proof assets, CTA, deadline, budget ceiling and success metric.
2. Strategist and trend scout research the angle and audience.
3. Copywriter, Reels creator and storyteller produce separate drafts.
4. Designer and video producer prepare visual / editing briefs using only supplied assets.
5. Content SEO checks discoverability and metadata.
6. Community manager prepares response templates and lead qualification flow.
7. QA checks factual claims, spelling, consent, personal data, prohibited promises and brand consistency.
8. Coordinator packages the approved content calendar and asks the owner to approve publication.
9. Analytics reports reach, clicks, conversations, qualified leads, booked jobs, revenue and cost per qualified lead where data is available.

## Initial content pillars

- Education: what PPF protects, film coverage, care, myths and limitations.
- Process proof: preparation, installation stages, edges, complex elements and quality control.
- Real cases: before/after only when genuine assets and permission are available.
- Vehicle-specific intent: model-focused examples and package explanations, with current approved prices only.
- Objection handling: durability, maintenance, washing, repair, coverage and value.
- Local trust: real workshop, team, location and service process.
- Conversion: consultation, quote request and inspection CTA.

## Suggested pilot cadence

Start with a two-week pilot, not a high-volume content flood:
- 3 educational / expert posts per week.
- 2 short vertical clips per week, only when usable footage exists.
- 1 verified case study per week.
- Stories / short updates on 3–5 days per week when real workshop material exists.
- One weekly performance review and content retro.

The cadence is a test hypothesis. Adjust using qualified enquiries and production capacity.

## Budget and permissions

The owner's total monthly ceiling is 30,000 RUB. It is a cap, not a spending instruction. Agent HQ model/API costs, paid tools, production costs and VK advertising must be tracked separately to avoid double counting.

Default permissions:
- Research, draft, plan, summarize and analyze: allowed within configured technical limits.
- Publish posts, upload media, reply to customers, send external messages, launch or edit ads, or spend money: approval required until specific integrations and permissions are configured and explicitly authorized.
- Never publish invented prices, warranties, certifications, customer testimonials, results or availability.
- Never expose customer personal data, faces, license plates or vehicle identifiers without consent.
- No fake engagement, spam, mass unsolicited messaging or competitor content copying.

## KPI hierarchy

Primary:
1. Qualified enquiries.
2. Booked inspections / quotes.
3. Paid jobs and revenue.
4. Cost per qualified enquiry and contribution margin, when costs are connected.

Secondary:
- Click-through rate to ppfprofi.ru / VK messages.
- Video retention and completion.
- Saves, shares and meaningful comments.
- Follower growth only as a supporting indicator.

Every report must state data source, date range, attribution limitations and missing instrumentation. Do not invent metrics.

## Technical status and next integrations

The worker role registry supports the new specialist roles, but workers are not yet connected to VK APIs, media generation / editing, analytics, CRM lead events or publication tools. Do not represent the team as operational until these integrations, isolated Agent HQ database access, budget configuration, worker deployment and end-to-end tests are completed.
